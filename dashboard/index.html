Data notes

    How the numbers work

    • Net PnL = realized PnL + fees + funding + rewards, all in USDT. Deposits, withdrawals and transfers between accounts are left out.
    • A trade is one realized PnL row. Its closing fee is the fee row in the same minute. Opening fees are counted in totals but can't be tied to a trade, because the export has no position ID.
    • Not in this export: symbol, side, leverage, entry and exit price. So there's no per-symbol or long/short split.
    • Returns are time-weighted on start-of-day balance, so deposits don't count as gains. Sharpe and Sortino are annualized with √365.
    • Everything is parsed in this browser tab. Your file is never uploaded.
